Recorded sound sources used in East Fort Drive

Engine and nearby traffic: "Car Engine Loop 96kHz, 4s" by qubodup.
https://opengameart.org/content/car-engine-loop-96khz-4s
Used under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Normalized WAV from the author's package. Playback pitch and gain vary in-game.
This is a generic car recording, not a Defender engine recording.

Rain: "Rain (loopable)" by Ylmir, first MP3 loop.
https://opengameart.org/content/rain-loopable
CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Volume changes between walking and driving.

Footsteps: "Footsteps" by GboxMikeFozzy, 01-footstep and 02-footstep.
https://opengameart.org/content/footsteps-0
CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Alternating recordings with subtle randomized pitch; cadence follows walking/running.

Horn, tire skid, door closing and impact Foley are original synthesized game effects, not field recordings. No claim is made that these recordings were captured in Trivandrum.
