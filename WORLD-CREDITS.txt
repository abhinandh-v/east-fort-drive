The complete OSM-derived database used by this game is supplied as region.json.
Projection, integer-metre rounding, road selection and building-height estimates
are made by this game. This derivative database is offered under ODbL 1.0.
Source query is included in source.zip as fetch-map.mjs; processing is prepare-map.mjs.
Data obtained through the public VK Maps Overpass mirror of OpenStreetMap.

Coverage: 10,000 metre radius around 8.48275 N, 76.94765 E (East Fort).
Road geometry and footprints are based on OSM; estimated heights, generated
facades, landmark details, road widths, vegetation and traffic are interpretations.
Terrain and bridges are flattened. Building interiors are not modeled.
Routing uses the connected road network in both directions for gameplay.
It is not a navigation service or an exact digital twin.
No Google tiles, Google imagery, Street View images or Google road database is included.

Lighting: Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy by Greg Zaal / Poly Haven, CC0.

The 1K HDR image is used for lighting/reflections, not as a Kerala landscape.
Defender-inspired vehicle and architectural details are original, unofficial models.
